Northern lights in Tromsø: frequently asked questions

Can you predict the northern lights three months ahead?

No. Aurora Planner uses short-range weather forecasts, usually up to around 16 days ahead. You can organize tours and cancellation deadlines months before your trip, but not get reliable daily weather predictions.

Does a clear-sky forecast guarantee northern lights?

No. Cloud cover is only one factor. Aurora visibility also depends on geomagnetic activity, darkness and local conditions.

Do I need travel dates to use Aurora Planner?

No. Explore upcoming nights without entering any dates. Trip planning and booking tracking are optional.

How far can an aurora tour travel from Tromsø?

The app compares areas based on approximate one-way driving ranges. Actual routes and travel times depend on the tour operator and road conditions.
